# Code of conduct

This project welcomes evidence, criticism, and disagreement about GFD. Participants must discuss the work without harassing, threatening, humiliating, or discriminating against other people.

## Expected conduct

- Critique claims, methods, evidence, and consequences rather than a person's motives or identity.
- Disclose uncertainty and correct material errors.
- Respect privacy, licenses, and source attribution.
- Do not fabricate results, identities, endorsements, or affiliations.
- Stop contact when another participant clearly asks you to stop.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, hate speech, doxxing, sexualized attention, threats, deliberate exposure of secrets, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

The project owner may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project. Decisions should identify the violated rule and remain proportionate to the conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive personal details in an issue. This initial personal project has no separate conduct committee.
