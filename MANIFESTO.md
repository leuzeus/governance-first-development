# Governance-First Development manifesto

This is an initial proposal. Its principles may change when evidence, counterexamples, or operating experience justify a revision.

1. Governance evolves with the code.
2. Every rule has an identifiable authority.
3. Intent, decision, implementation, test, and evidence remain distinct.
4. A rigorous result does not require every procedure to be rigid.
5. Controls match the risk and context of a change.
6. An AI agent cannot silently grant itself new authority.
7. A repository remains understandable without private conversational memory.
8. Governance is open to audit.
9. Procedural compliance does not replace a valid result.
10. The cost of governance is evaluated with its value.
11. Exceptions are explicit, bounded, reversible, and time-limited when appropriate.
12. Humans retain the responsibilities and authorities defined by the project.
13. Evidence supports named claims and no more.
14. Documentary evidence does not replace material evidence when the claim requires it.
15. Governance can change without erasing its history.
16. A product is never blamed for a test that did not run.
